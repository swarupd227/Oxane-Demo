(f) the grant of any Lien that is a Permitted Lien, and the transfer of any property to the Lender or to any person at the direction of the Lender in the exercise of its remedies under Section 8.2;

(g) the disposition of cash and cash equivalents, and the application of funds held in the Collection Account, in each case in accordance with Section 2.10 or, after an Event of Default, Section 8.3;

(h) the disposition of obsolete, worn-out or surplus equipment and property that is not Collateral and is not material to the business of the Borrower, provided that the aggregate fair market value of property disposed of under this paragraph in any fiscal year does not exceed {{threshold_amount}};

(i) the transfer of any Receivable, together with all related rights, to a successor Servicer or to a custodian solely for purposes of servicing, safekeeping or enforcement, provided that the Lien of the Lender is not impaired and the transferee acknowledges the interest of the Lender in writing; and

(j) any Disposition consented to in writing by the Lender, but only to the extent and on the conditions specified in that consent.

The Borrower shall not make any Disposition permitted by this Section to any Affiliate except on terms that comply with Section 6.7 and that are consistent with the arm's length standard in that Section. The Borrower shall not sell or otherwise transfer any Receivable that is, on the date of the Disposition, included in the Pool, other than in accordance with paragraphs (a) through (j) above, and any purported sale in breach of this Section is void as against the Borrower to the extent permitted by applicable law.

(a) Each Disposition permitted under paragraph (a) above shall be accompanied by a certificate of a Responsible Officer of the Borrower delivered to the Lender before the Disposition, stating the Receivables to be disposed of, the proceeds and the proposed application of the proceeds, and confirming, with supporting calculations, that the conditions to the Disposition under that paragraph are satisfied. The Lender shall within a reasonable period after receipt of such certificate release its Lien on the Receivables concerned, upon receipt of the proceeds, to the extent required by Section 3.3.

(b) The selection of Receivables to be disposed of under paragraph (a) above shall not be made in a manner that is intended to be adverse to the interests of the Lender, and in particular shall not be made by selecting Receivables that are more likely to be Eligible Receivables or have a lower risk of default than the remaining Pool Receivables unless the Borrower has demonstrated to the satisfaction of the Lender that the Borrowing Base after giving effect to the Disposition is no less than it would have been had a random selection been made.

(c) No Disposition shall be permitted by reason of any provision of this Section if, at the time of or after giving effect to it, a Delinquency Trigger is continuing, except under paragraphs (b), (c), (e) and (f) above and under paragraph (a) above where the proceeds are applied as required by Section 2.10 as if the Revolving Period had ended.

(d) The Borrower acknowledges that a Disposition, other than a Disposition permitted by this Section, would be an infringement of the Lien of the Lender and agrees to hold in trust for the Lender, and to pay into the Collection Account on demand, all proceeds of any Disposition made in breach of this Section.

## 6.5 Investments
The Borrower shall not make, purchase, acquire or hold any loan, advance, capital contribution, stock, bond, note or other security of, or any interest in, any person, or make any other investment of any kind, other than (a) Receivables acquired under the Purchase Agreement, (b) cash and cash equivalents held in the Collection Account or in other accounts permitted by Section 6.10, invested only in instruments of the kind approved in writing by the Lender, (c) rights under the Transaction Documents and under any hedging arrangement permitted by Section 5.13, and (d) trade credit and prepayments to service providers extended in the ordinary course of business. The Borrower shall not form or acquire any Subsidiary, or acquire any equity interest in any person, other than as permitted by Section 6.14.

## 6.6 Restricted Payments
The Borrower shall not declare, pay or make, directly or indirectly, any dividend or distribution on, or any payment on account of the purchase, redemption, retirement or other acquisition of, any of its equity interests, any payment of principal or interest on any Debt subordinated to the Obligations, or any payment of any management, advisory or similar fee to the Parent or any Affiliate (each, a "Restricted Payment"), other than Restricted Payments made on a Payment Date, from funds available for that purpose under Section 2.10, if and only if, both immediately before and immediately after giving effect to it, (a) no Event of Default, Delinquency Trigger or Servicer Default has occurred and is continuing, (b) no Borrowing Base Deficiency exists, (c) the Borrower is Solvent and its ability to pay its debts as they fall due is not impaired, and (d) the Borrower has delivered the Borrowing Base Certificate and the Servicer Report most recently required by Schedule 3 and has paid in full all fees, interest and other amounts then due to the Lender and the Verification Agent. Any Restricted Payment in breach of this Section shall be held by the recipient in trust for the Lender and returned to the Borrower or paid into the Collection Account on demand.

## 6.7 Transactions with Affiliates
The Borrower shall not enter into, or be a party to, any transaction with any Affiliate, including the Parent, any Originator and the Servicer, or with any director, officer, manager or equity holder of the Borrower or any of its Affiliates, except (a) transactions expressly contemplated by the Transaction Documents, (b) transactions in the ordinary course of business and pursuant to the reasonable requirements of the business of the Borrower, which are on terms and conditions fair and reasonable to the Borrower and no less favorable to the Borrower than would be obtained in a comparable arm's length transaction with a person that is not an Affiliate, and (c) the payment of reasonable compensation and indemnity to the independent manager of the Borrower.

Each such transaction, other than those under the Transaction Documents, shall be recorded in writing. The Borrower shall not enter into any transaction that has the effect of making the Borrower liable for any obligation of the Parent or any Affiliate, nor shall the Borrower lend money to, or guarantee the obligations of, any Affiliate.

## 6.8 Business Activities
The Borrower shall not engage in any business or activity other than the purchase, ownership, financing, servicing through the Servicer, administration and enforcement of Receivables and the Collateral, the borrowing of Advances, and such other activities as are necessary, convenient or incidental to those activities and as are permitted by its constitutional documents and the Transaction Documents. The Borrower shall not own any asset other than the Receivables, the related Collateral, its rights under the Transaction Documents and cash and cash equivalents in permitted accounts, shall not have any employees, and shall not enter into any agreement other than the Transaction Documents and agreements incidental to its permitted activities, such as agreements for legal, audit, administrative and custodial services.

## 6.9 Amendments to Documents
The Borrower shall not, and shall not permit the Parent, any Originator or the Servicer to, amend, supplement, waive any provision of, terminate or replace, or consent to any departure from, the Purchase Agreement, the Servicing Agreement, the constitutional documents of the Borrower, any custody or lockbox agreement, any control agreement or any document governing any Subordinated Debt, in each case without the prior written consent of the Lender, other than an amendment of a purely administrative or technical nature that is not adverse to the Lender and that has been notified to the Lender in writing before it takes effect.

The Borrower shall deliver to the Lender a copy of each amendment, supplement and waiver, whether or not consented to, promptly after it takes effect.

## 6.10 Change of Accounts
The Borrower shall not, and shall not permit the Servicer to, add or terminate any bank or lockbox account into which Collections are or may be deposited, close or change the Collection Account, or make or consent to any change in the payment instructions given to Obligors, in each case without the prior written consent of the Lender, which consent shall be given or withheld in the sole discretion of the Lender where such change is to an account not subject to a control agreement in favor of the Lender.

The Borrower shall maintain no deposit account or securities account other than the Collection Account and accounts that are subject to a control agreement in favor of the Lender and, except where the Lender otherwise agrees, shall not permit any amount other than Collections and proceeds of Advances to be deposited into any such account.

## 6.11 Credit Policy Changes
The Borrower shall not, and shall not permit the Servicer or any Originator to, amend, modify or waive any provision of the Credit and Collection Policy, or change in any material respect the manner in which Receivables are originated, underwritten, serviced, collected or charged off, unless (a) the Lender has given its prior written consent, such consent not to be unreasonably withheld in the case of a change that, in the reasonable judgment of the Lender, would not adversely affect the collectability of the Pool Receivables or the interests of the Lender, or (b) the change is required by applicable law, in which case the Borrower shall give the Lender prompt written notice of it, including a description of the legal requirement. Any change to the definition of delinquency, charge-off or modification standards, or to the method of calculating the Outstanding Balance of any Receivable, is deemed material for the purposes of this Section.

## 6.13 Sanctions
The Borrower shall not, and shall procure that the Parent, the Servicer and each Originator and their respective directors, officers, employees and agents do not, directly or indirectly, (a) become, or be owned or controlled by, a person that is the subject of sanctions administered by the United States government, (b) engage in any dealing or transaction with, or for the benefit of, any such person or any person located, organized or resident in a Restricted Jurisdiction, (c) include in the Pool any Receivable owed by an Obligor that is such a person or whose billing address is in a Restricted Jurisdiction, or (d) use any proceeds of any Advance or any Collections, or lend, contribute or otherwise make them available to any person, in any manner that would result in a violation of sanctions or anti-corruption laws by any party to the Transaction Documents. The Borrower shall promptly notify the Lender upon becoming aware of any breach or suspected breach of this Section.

## 6.14 Subsidiaries
The Borrower shall not form or acquire any Subsidiary, make any investment in any Subsidiary, or hold any equity interest in any person. The Borrower shall not become a member of any partnership, joint venture or similar arrangement, or enter into any agreement for the sharing of profits or losses with any person other than under the Transaction Documents.

## 6.15 Fiscal Year
The Borrower shall not change its fiscal year or the basis on which it prepares its financial statements or accounts, and shall not permit the Parent to change its fiscal year, in each case without the prior written consent of the Lender, other than a change to a fiscal year that ends on a different date but that the Parent has notified to the Lender at least 60 days before the change and in respect of which the Parent has agreed in writing to such amendments to Schedule 3 and Section 6.12 as the Lender may reasonably require to preserve the intent of those provisions.

## 7.2 Servicer Termination
Each of the following is a "Servicer Default", and the Lender may, by notice to the Borrower and the Servicer, at any time after the occurrence of a Servicer Default and while it is continuing, terminate all of the rights and obligations of the Servicer under the Servicing Agreement, whereupon the termination takes effect on the date specified in the notice:

(a) the Servicer fails to deposit, or to cause to be deposited, any Collections into the Collection Account, or to make any payment or transfer required by the Servicing Agreement, when due, and the failure continues for two Business Days;

(b) the Servicer fails to deliver any Servicer Report or other report required by Section 5.5, or to give any notice required by Section 5.6, within two Business Days after the date on which it is required;

(c) the Servicer fails to observe or perform any other covenant or agreement in the Servicing Agreement or any other Transaction Document to which it is a party, and the failure continues for 30 days after the earlier of notice from the Lender and actual knowledge of a Responsible Officer of the Servicer;

(d) any representation, warranty or certification made by the Servicer in any Transaction Document or in any report or certificate delivered under it proves to have been incorrect in any material respect when made or deemed made;

(e) the Servicer becomes subject to an Insolvency Event, or admits in writing its inability to pay its debts generally as they become due;

(f) the Servicer ceases to hold any license or qualification required to service the Pool Receivables, or becomes subject to any order or regulatory action of a governmental authority that prohibits or materially restricts it from servicing the Pool Receivables;

(g) a Change of Control of the Servicer occurs;

(h) a Material Adverse Effect occurs in relation to the Servicer or its ability to service the Pool; or

(i) the Servicer, as a result of a final audit or field examination under Section 5.4, is found to have materially misapplied or failed to segregate any Collections, or to have failed to maintain records sufficient to permit the verification of the Pool.

The Servicer Default described in paragraph (e) above takes effect as a termination of the Servicer immediately, without the need for notice, to the extent permitted by applicable law. The Servicer shall cooperate fully with the Lender and any successor in effecting any termination, and the Borrower shall not waive any Servicer Default without the prior written consent of the Lender.

## 7.3 Replacement Servicer
Upon the termination of the Servicer under Section 7.2, or upon the resignation of the Servicer where permitted by the Servicing Agreement, the Lender may appoint a successor servicer, which may be the Lender or an Affiliate of the Lender or any other person that the Lender, in its sole discretion, considers qualified, to assume the duties of the Servicer under the Servicing Agreement on the terms of that Agreement or on such other terms as the Lender and the successor may agree, subject to the compensation of the successor not exceeding that which is customary for servicing receivables of the same kind.

(a) The outgoing Servicer shall continue to perform its functions as Servicer until the date of the transfer of servicing, shall transfer to the successor all records, data, files, certificates of title, powers of attorney and other property relating to the Pool, and shall give the successor full access to its premises, systems and personnel for transition purposes.

(b) The outgoing Servicer shall, and the Borrower shall procure that it does, deliver notice to each Obligor and each Dealer, in the form directed by the Lender, of the change in servicer and of the new payment instructions, and shall not permit any Collections to be paid to any account other than the Collection Account.

(c) The Borrower shall pay, or reimburse the Lender for, the reasonable costs and expenses of the transfer of servicing, to the extent not paid by the outgoing Servicer under the Servicing Agreement, in accordance with the priority of payments in Section 2.10 or, after an Event of Default, Section 8.3.

(d) The Borrower shall and shall procure that the Parent and each Originator shall execute such documents and take such further action as the Lender may reasonably request to enable the successor to perform its obligations.

(e) No successor servicer shall have any liability for any act or omission of the predecessor, and the termination of the Servicer does not affect any liability of the Servicer in respect of any period before the effective date of termination.

## 7.4 Cash Dominion
From the date on which any of the following occurs and for so long as it is continuing, the Lender may, by notice to the Borrower and the Servicer, and the Borrower shall be treated as having authorized the Lender, to exercise exclusive dominion and control over the Collection Account and all Collections (a "Cash Dominion Event"): (a) an Event of Default, (b) a Servicer Default, (c) a Delinquency Trigger, or (d) a Borrowing Base Deficiency that has not been cured within the time permitted by Section 2.4.

(a) During a Cash Dominion Event, all Collections and other amounts in the Collection Account shall be applied solely by the Lender in accordance with Section 2.10, or, if an Event of Default has occurred and is continuing and the Lender has exercised its remedies, in accordance with Section 8.3, and the Borrower shall have no right to give any instruction with respect to, or to withdraw any amount from, the Collection Account.

(b) The Lender may, and the Borrower irrevocably authorizes and directs the Lender to, give notice to each depositary bank and to each Obligor of the exercise of control, to direct payments to the account specified by the Lender, and to endorse in the name of the Borrower any check or other instrument for the payment of money received in respect of the Collateral.

(c) During a Cash Dominion Event the Borrower shall not be entitled to make any Restricted Payment, and no Advance shall be made except to the extent expressly permitted by the Lender in writing after a Delinquency Trigger has ceased to continue.

(d) The exercise of rights under this Section does not constitute an acceptance of any breach, a waiver of any Event of Default, or an exercise of remedies under Section 8.2, and shall be without prejudice to any other right of the Lender.

## 7.5 Effect of Cure
A Delinquency Trigger, a Cash Dominion Event or a Servicer Default shall be treated as no longer continuing only to the extent provided in this Section.

(a) A Delinquency Trigger ceases to be continuing when the test in Section 7.1 is no longer met, as shown in a Servicer Report delivered under Section 5.5 and confirmed by the Verification Agent, and, upon that cessation, the suspension of the Revolving Period under Section 7.1 shall end, subject to the Lender having received written confirmation from the Verification Agent and to the satisfaction of each condition in Section 3.2 for the next Advance.

(b) A Servicer Default is cured only if it is remedied to the satisfaction of the Lender, which shall have determined by written notice to the Borrower and the Servicer that it has been remedied, and a termination of the Servicer under Section 7.2 that has taken effect shall not be reversed by the later cure of the Servicer Default.

(c) A Cash Dominion Event ends when each circumstance that gave rise to it has ceased and the Lender has given written notice of that fact to the Borrower, and any Collections applied during the period of the Cash Dominion Event under Section 2.10 shall not be re-advanced to the Borrower except as provided in Section 2.2 and subject to the conditions in Section 3.2.

(d) No cure under this Section shall (i) waive or cure any Event of Default that has occurred, or (ii) operate to restore the Revolving Period if the Commitment has terminated, and the Lender shall have no obligation to make any Advance during any period when any of the circumstances referred to in this Section is continuing, even if the Borrower alleges that a cure has taken place, unless the Lender has confirmed the cure in writing.
