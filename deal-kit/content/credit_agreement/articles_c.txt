## 2.2 Advances and Borrowing Procedure
(a) Advance Requests. The Borrower may request an Advance by delivering to the Lender, with a copy to the Verification Agent, a request substantially in the form of Exhibit B (an "Advance Request"), signed by a Responsible Officer of the Borrower, not later than the time and on the number of Business Days before the proposed date of Advance that the Lender may from time to time specify to the Borrower in writing. Each Advance Request is irrevocable once delivered, shall specify the proposed date of the Advance, which must be a Business Day falling within the Revolving Period, and the principal amount requested, and shall be accompanied by a Borrowing Base Certificate and a Loan Tape each prepared as of a date not earlier than the date the Lender reasonably requires for that purpose, together with a schedule identifying each Receivable that the Borrower proposes to include in the Pool in connection with that Advance.

(b) Conditions. The Lender is not obliged to make an Advance unless each condition set out in Section 3.2 is satisfied on the date of the Advance Request and on the proposed date of the Advance, and the Borrower, by delivering an Advance Request, represents and warrants to the Lender that those conditions are then satisfied and will remain satisfied immediately after giving effect to the Advance and the application of its proceeds. Without limiting the foregoing, no Advance shall be made if, immediately after giving effect to it, the aggregate outstanding principal amount of the Advances would exceed the lesser of the Facility Limit and the Borrowing Base, or if a Borrowing Base Deficiency, a Delinquency Trigger, an Event of Default, or an event that with notice or lapse of time or both would constitute an Event of Default, is then continuing.

(c) Funding. Subject to paragraphs (a) and (b) above, the Lender shall make the proceeds of each Advance available to the Borrower on the proposed date of the Advance by crediting the account of the Borrower that is designated in the Advance Request and that is a Collection Account or another account of the Borrower approved in writing by the Lender. The Lender may, but is not obliged to, rely on any Advance Request that it in good faith believes to have been given by a Responsible Officer, and the Borrower shall indemnify the Lender against any loss or expense incurred in reliance on a request so given, whether or not the request was in fact authorised.

(d) Records. The Lender shall record in its books the date and amount of each Advance and of each payment of principal, interest and other amounts in respect of the Advances. Those records are, in the absence of manifest error, conclusive evidence of the amounts owing under this Agreement, but a failure by the Lender to record, or an error in recording, any amount does not affect the obligation of the Borrower to pay the Obligations in accordance with the Transaction Documents. If the Lender so requests, the Borrower shall execute and deliver a promissory note evidencing the Advances, in a form reasonably acceptable to the Lender.

## 2.4 Borrowing Base Deficiency
(a) Notice and cure. If at any time a Borrowing Base Deficiency exists, whether as shown in a Borrowing Base Certificate, in a report of the Verification Agent, or by reason of a recalculation by the Lender under Section 3.4, the Borrower shall, on or before the third Business Day after the earlier of (i) receipt of notice of the Borrowing Base Deficiency from the Lender and (ii) the date on which a Responsible Officer of the Borrower or of the Servicer first has knowledge of it (the "Cure Period"), eliminate the Borrowing Base Deficiency in full by taking one or more of the actions described in paragraph (b) below. The Borrower shall give the Lender written notice of any Borrowing Base Deficiency promptly, and in any event within one Business Day, after a Responsible Officer of the Borrower first obtains knowledge of it.

(b) Methods of cure. The Borrower may cure a Borrowing Base Deficiency (i) by prepaying the Advances, together with accrued interest on the amount prepaid and any amounts payable under Section 2.12 in connection with that prepayment, in an amount sufficient to ensure that, after giving effect to the prepayment, no Borrowing Base Deficiency exists, or (ii) by transferring to the Pool additional Receivables that are Eligible Receivables and are acceptable to the Lender, in each case as confirmed in a new Borrowing Base Certificate delivered to the Lender and the Verification Agent, or (iii) by any combination of the foregoing; provided that no Receivable shall be treated as added to the Pool for this purpose until the Lender has received the documents and confirmations that Section 3.2 would require in connection with an Advance secured by that Receivable.

(c) Restrictions during the Cure Period. From the date on which a Borrowing Base Deficiency first exists until it has been cured in full, (i) the Lender has no obligation to make any Advance, (ii) the Borrower shall not request, and shall not be entitled to receive, the release of any Collateral or any Collections under Section 3.3 or Section 2.10, except to the extent the release would not cause or increase a Borrowing Base Deficiency, and (iii) the Lender may, by notice to the Borrower and the Servicer, require that all Collections be applied in accordance with Section 2.10 as though the Revolving Period had ended.

(d) Consequence of failure to cure. If a Borrowing Base Deficiency has not been eliminated in full by the end of the Cure Period, the failure constitutes an Event of Default under Section 8.1(c) without any further notice, grace period or demand. The Lender may then exercise any right or remedy available to it under Section 8.2, and the continuing existence of the Borrowing Base Deficiency shall not be waived by any delay or forbearance of the Lender short of a written waiver signed by it.

(e) No limitation. Cure of a Borrowing Base Deficiency under this Section does not constitute a waiver of any other Event of Default, and does not oblige the Lender to make any Advance that would not otherwise be available under this Agreement.

## 2.5 Prepayments and Commitment Reduction
(a) Voluntary prepayment. The Borrower may prepay the Advances in whole or in part on any Business Day on not less than the number of Business Days' prior written notice to the Lender that the Lender specifies from time to time. Each notice of prepayment is irrevocable unless it states that it is conditional upon the occurrence of an event specified in the notice, in which case it may be revoked if that event does not occur, and the Borrower shall pay on demand any loss or expense that the Lender incurs as a result of the revocation or of any prepayment made on a day other than the last day of an Interest Period, as certified by the Lender in reasonable detail. Each prepayment shall be accompanied by all interest accrued on the amount prepaid to the date of prepayment.

(b) Mandatory prepayment. The Borrower shall prepay the Advances, in the amount and at the time indicated, (i) under Section 2.4, to the extent necessary to cure a Borrowing Base Deficiency, (ii) on the Final Maturity Date, in an amount equal to all Advances then outstanding, (iii) if the Commitment is reduced or terminated, to the extent that the aggregate outstanding principal amount of the Advances would otherwise exceed the Commitment as so reduced, and (iv) on receipt, in the amount of any proceeds of a sale, disposition or other realisation of Collateral that is not permitted by this Agreement, or that, though permitted, is not replaced by Collateral of equal or greater value in accordance with this Agreement.

(c) Reborrowing. Amounts prepaid during the Revolving Period under paragraph (a) above may be reborrowed in accordance with Section 2.1 and Section 2.2. Amounts prepaid after the Revolving Period has ended or while it is suspended, and amounts prepaid under paragraph (b) above, may not be reborrowed except to the extent the Lender otherwise agrees in writing.

(d) Commitment reduction. The Borrower may, on not less than the notice to the Lender that the Lender specifies from time to time, permanently reduce the unused portion of the Commitment, in whole or in part, provided that no reduction shall result in the Commitment being less than the aggregate outstanding principal amount of the Advances unless the Borrower prepays the Advances at the same time. Any reduction is applied to the Commitment set out in Annex A and, once made, may not be reinstated. The Commitment terminates automatically on the Revolving Period End Date, and the unused portion of the Commitment on that date is cancelled.

## 2.10 Collections and Application of Funds
(a) Collections. The Borrower shall cause all Collections to be paid into the Collection Account in the manner required by Section 5.12, and the Borrower and the Servicer shall hold in trust for the Lender any Collections that they receive until those Collections have been so deposited. The Collection Account is a segregated account in the name of the Borrower over which the Lender has control, and no person other than the Lender may withdraw funds from it except as permitted by this Section and Section 7.4.

(b) Revolving Period. Provided that no Borrowing Base Deficiency, Delinquency Trigger or Event of Default, and no event that with notice or lapse of time would be an Event of Default, is continuing, and subject to Section 7.4, amounts on deposit in the Collection Account on any Business Day during the Revolving Period that represent Collections may be applied by the Borrower first to the payments due on the next Payment Date in the order set out in steps (a) through (d) of the waterfall in paragraph (c) below, and any remaining Collections may be applied, at the direction of the Borrower, to the purchase of additional Receivables that become Eligible Receivables upon their inclusion in the Pool, or released to the Borrower, in each case if and to the extent that Availability is not less than zero after giving effect to that application or release.

(c) Waterfall. On each Payment Date, and on any other date on which the Lender requires application under Section 2.4(c), Section 7.1 or Section 7.4, and at all times after the Revolving Period has ended or while it is suspended, the Lender (or the Servicer at the direction of the Lender) shall apply all amounts on deposit in the Collection Account representing Collections, and all other amounts available for the purpose, in the following order of priority:

(a) first, to the payment of all taxes, fees, costs, expenses and indemnities then due and payable to the Lender and the Administrative Agent under the Transaction Documents, including amounts payable under Section 10.3 and Section 10.4;

(b) second, to the payment of the fees, costs and expenses then due to the Verification Agent under Section 9.10, to the extent not paid under paragraph (a) above;

(c) third, to the payment of any servicing fee then due to the Servicer, provided that if a Servicer Default has occurred and is continuing, that payment shall be made only to the extent the Lender approves;

(d) fourth, to the payment of accrued and unpaid interest on the Advances and of all other fees due under the Fee Letter, including each Unused Fee;

(e) fifth, to the payment of the principal of the Advances in an amount equal to any Borrowing Base Deficiency then existing;

(g) seventh, to the payment of all other Obligations then due and payable; and

(h) eighth, any remainder, to the Borrower or as the Borrower directs, subject to Section 8.3 if an Event of Default is continuing.

(d) Shortfalls. If amounts available on any date are insufficient to pay in full all amounts due under any step of the waterfall, those amounts shall be applied to that step pro rata among the persons entitled to them, according to the amounts then due, and no step shall be paid until all prior steps have been paid in full.

(e) Misdirected funds. Any amount received by the Borrower, the Parent or the Servicer that constitutes Collections and is not deposited in the Collection Account on the day of receipt is held in trust for the Lender, shall be segregated from the property of the recipient, and shall be paid into the Collection Account on the next Business Day. The Lender may at any time give notice to any Obligor or Dealer directing it to make payments directly to the Collection Account, and the Borrower and the Servicer irrevocably authorise it to do so.

## 2.11 Benchmark Replacement
(a) Benchmark Transition. If the Lender determines, in good faith and after consultation with the Borrower, that (i) adequate and reasonable means do not exist for ascertaining Term SOFR for an Interest Period, (ii) the administrator of Term SOFR or a governmental authority having jurisdiction over that administrator has made a public statement that Term SOFR is no longer representative or will cease to be provided, or (iii) Term SOFR for an Interest Period will not adequately and fairly reflect the cost to the Lender of making or maintaining the Advances (each a "Benchmark Transition Event"), then the Lender shall promptly notify the Borrower of the Benchmark Transition Event and the provisions of this Section shall apply.

(b) Replacement rate. From the date of a Benchmark Transition Event, the interest rate on the Advances shall be determined by reference to a replacement benchmark selected by the Lender, giving due consideration to any benchmark selected or recommended by the relevant governmental body or then prevailing in the market for comparable secured warehouse facilities denominated in the same currency, together with any spread adjustment that the Lender determines is appropriate to preserve, so far as practicable, the economic equivalence of the interest rate before and after the replacement (the "Benchmark Replacement").

(c) Conforming changes. The Lender may from time to time make technical, administrative or operational changes to this Agreement that it considers appropriate to implement the Benchmark Replacement, including changes to the definition of Interest Period, the timing and frequency of determining rates and making payments of interest, the lookback or rounding conventions, and the treatment of non-Business Days. Those changes become effective without any further action or consent of the Borrower on notice from the Lender.

(d) Interim period. Until a Benchmark Replacement has taken effect, the Lender's obligation to make or maintain Advances that bear interest by reference to Term SOFR is suspended, and any Advance outstanding shall bear interest at a rate per annum that is the greater of the rate determined by the Lender as reflecting its cost of funds and the Margin, in either case determined in good faith and notified to the Borrower.

## 9.8 Verification Agent
(a) Appointment. The Lender has appointed, and may from time to time replace, an independent third party to act as the Verification Agent, on terms that the Lender determines in a separate engagement letter between the Lender and the Verification Agent. The Borrower consents to that appointment and to every replacement, and shall, and shall cause the Servicer and the Parent to, cooperate with the Verification Agent in the performance of its duties. The Lender shall notify the Borrower in writing of the identity of the Verification Agent, and of any replacement, promptly after the appointment takes effect.

(b) Function. The role of the Verification Agent is to check each Borrowing Base Certificate delivered under this Agreement against the Loan Tape delivered with it and against this Agreement, and to report to the Lender on the results. In performing that role, the Verification Agent shall verify, on the basis of the Loan Tape and the books and records of the Borrower and the Servicer to which it is given access, (i) that the data in the Borrowing Base Certificate agree with the data in the Loan Tape, (ii) that each Receivable included in the Eligible Pool Balance satisfies the criteria set out in Schedule 1, (iii) that the Excess Concentration Amount has been determined in accordance with Schedule 2, and (iv) that the calculation of the Borrowing Base follows the definitions and formulas in this Agreement, including Section 2.3, and is arithmetically correct.

(c) Access and information. The Borrower shall, and shall cause the Servicer to, give the Verification Agent prompt access during normal business hours to the Loan Tape, the Servicer Reports, the files relating to the Receivables, and the personnel of the Borrower and the Servicer, and shall provide any information, documents and explanations that the Verification Agent reasonably requests in connection with its function.

(d) No duty to the Borrower. The Verification Agent acts solely on behalf of the Lender. It owes no duty to the Borrower, the Parent, the Servicer or any other person, and none of them may rely on any report or finding of the Verification Agent. The verification does not relieve the Borrower of its responsibility for the accuracy of each Borrowing Base Certificate and each Loan Tape, and does not constitute a representation, warranty or waiver by the Lender.

(e) Replacement and removal. The Lender may remove the Verification Agent at any time, with or without cause, by notice to it, and may appoint a successor on the terms provided in paragraph (a) above. Until a successor takes up the appointment, the Lender may itself perform the functions described in paragraph (b) above, and any determination made by the Lender in the interim has the same effect under this Agreement as a determination of the Verification Agent.

## 9.9 Reports of the Verification Agent
(a) Reports. The Verification Agent shall deliver to the Lender, in writing, a report on each Borrowing Base Certificate that it has reviewed, within the time and in the form that the Lender requires, stating whether the Borrowing Base Certificate agrees with the Loan Tape and with this Agreement and identifying each exception, discrepancy or recalculation, including any Receivable that it considers does not satisfy a criterion in Schedule 1, any category in which it considers a limit in Schedule 2 is exceeded, and any difference between the amount of the Borrowing Base shown in the Borrowing Base Certificate and the amount that it has calculated.

(b) Effect of reports. For the purposes of the definition of Borrowing Base and the determinations contemplated by Section 3.4, the Borrowing Base shown in a Borrowing Base Certificate is treated as verified only to the extent confirmed in the report of the Verification Agent. Where the Verification Agent reports a different amount, the amount determined by the Lender after taking the report into account, and after affording the Borrower a reasonable opportunity to comment, is the Borrowing Base for the purposes of this Agreement, absent manifest error.

(c) Copies to the Borrower. The Lender may, but is not obliged to, provide a copy of any report of the Verification Agent, or a summary of its findings, to the Borrower or the Servicer.
